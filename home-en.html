The beacon Venerable Elder Zongcai built

In 2003, Venerable Elder Zongcai of Pure Land Temple in Los Angeles founded the organization that is today Beacon Star Foundation. He practiced with rare diligence all his life, living alone in a mountain hut for two or three decades without once coming down. He lived simply and wasted nothing. He was warm, easy to talk to, and never acted like anyone important.

In 2022 the Elder passed away peacefully, at the age of 103.

After his passing, the organization faced dissolution. Its administrator, the lay devotee Yanjing, asked Venerable Xingren Shi to take it on.

“Then let me be the one who carries the flame onward, and let all of us keep this lamp burning together.” — Venerable Xingren Shi

To carry on what the Elder left us, the English name was kept: Beacon Star Foundation. To open a new hope for what comes next, the Chinese name became Xingxing Zhi Huo (星星之火) — “a single spark.”
